# Origins

### My homeland is Da Nang,

I am a Vietnamese, born and raised in the coastal city of Da Nang,
a place shaped by the sea, mountains and modern. Quiet enough to
reflect and open enough to dream.


### currently live in Ho Chi Minh city,...

When I entered university, I carried a clear sense of where I
thought I was headed. The reality, however, turned out differently.
My first two years did not go as planned, and at times, I found myself
drifting away from the path I had imagined. Lost and falling deep.


### with the world ahead,...

Spring comes after winter. I begin again, clearer in direction and
steadier in purpose. I am working toward a future where I can earn my
dream job, continue academic path through studying abroad, and expand
my perspective through the world itself.


### a place to write and learn.
This blog marks a point of transition. It is not a record of failure,
but a space for reflection and intention. Here, I write to make sense of
what I am learning, to think more carefully about where I am going, and
to move forward with greater clarity. One step at a time.
